## The "enclave" argument (verbatim, condensed)

- "This is the grid inside the grid: a layer of firm, privately secured electricity built for facilities that cannot tolerate interruption, operating within a public system still struggling to expand transmission and connect new generation."

- Demands listed: disclose electricity/water/land/tax/guarantee arrangements; new power procurement must add generation rather than reserve existing capacity; contracts should require shared-grid investment, local compute access, skills partnerships; backup diesel use "visible rather than buried in technical filings".
