## Continent 8 enters African market with Joburg data centre

There’s a new arrival in the African data centre market as Continent 8 Technologies, a provider of managed technology solutions designed for the global iGaming and online sports betting industry, announces its expansion into Africa with the launch of a new data centre in South Africa.

The company says the deployment, in Johannesburg extends its full portfolio of managed technology, connectivity, cloud and cybersecurity solutions into the region, supported by its purpose-built private global network and world-class digital infrastructure.

The Johannesburg facility marks Continent 8’s first deployment on the African continent and will enable operators, platform providers and content suppliers to deliver faster, more consistent digital experiences to South African players while remaining seamlessly connected with their wider international operations and partners. It will be live in Q4 2026.

As the company explains, South Africa is one of the world’s highest-volume regulated betting markets, and therefore requires infrastructure capable of supporting substantial transaction volumes with low latency and high resilience.

Located close to market and directly connected to Continent 8’s private global backbone, the new facility will enable customers to leverage the same highly resilient infrastructure used across more than 100 connected locations worldwide.

The Johannesburg data centre will provide Tier III infrastructure within an ISO 27001-certified environment and deliver Continent 8’s full portfolio of managed technology services, including colocation, managed infrastructure, cloud connectivity, content delivery, DDoS protection and managed cybersecurity.

Customers will also gain access to Continent 8’s private global network, and benefit from direct connectivity to leading hyperscale cloud providers including AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure.
