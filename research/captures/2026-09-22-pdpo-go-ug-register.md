## Take Action

Protect your data privacy rights with these essential actions

## PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION OFFICE (PDPO) PORTAL PRIVACY NOTICE

The Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) is committed to safeguarding your personal data in line with the _Data Protection and Privacy Act, Cap. 97_ and its Regulations.

## Purpose of This Notice

This Privacy Notice outlines how the PDPO collects, uses, shares, and secures your personal data when you interact with the office, whether through the website or in person.

## Your Rights and Legal Basis

This Privacy Notice explains your rights as a data subject, as well as the legal basis for collecting and processing your personal data by PDPO.
