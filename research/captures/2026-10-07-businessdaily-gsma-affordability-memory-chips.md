## Figures in the article (attributions as stated in the piece)

- GSMA + Partnership for Digital Access in Africa (PDAA) study, Kenya adult population: "among Kenya's adult urban population, 68 percent use mobile internet, while 27 percent are aware of it but do not use it and six percent remain unaware. In rural areas, 49 percent use mobile internet, 38 percent know about it but do not use it, and 13 percent remain unaware."
- Barrier structure: handset cost is "the biggest reported barrier in both urban and rural areas" for the aware-but-offline; data cost is the biggest obstacle to more use among existing users.
- THE AI-DATA-CENTRE ANGLE: "Global smartphone prices are facing pressure from a shortage of memory chips, with manufacturers prioritising supply for high-margin artificial intelligence (AI) data centres."
- "Global smartphone shipments fell 11 percent year-on-year in the second quarter of 2026 to their lowest second-quarter level since 2013, according to the global market intelligence firm Counterpoint Research." Rising memory costs hit entry-level and mid-range devices hardest.
- World Bank 2025 analysis: "Kenya's mobile market is highly concentrated and linked this structure to higher data prices and lower usage than some regional peers such as Ghana, Nigeria, Rwanda and Zambia."
- CA of Kenya data (June 2026): "mobile data subscriptions had reached 64.3 million, up 9.7 percent from 58.6 million a year earlier. Mobile broadband accounted for 85.5 percent of total subscriptions, with 4G accounting for 48.3 million. 5G subscriptions more than doubled over the year to 2.1 million."
- CA quote: "The continued shift towards internet-based communication and digital content, including over-the-top and video-on-demand services, is expected to drive demand for broadband connectivity and active bandwidth capacity."
