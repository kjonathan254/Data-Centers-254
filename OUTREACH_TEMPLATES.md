## Phone Call Script (Initial Outreach)

### Opening (30 seconds)
"Good [morning/afternoon], my name is [Your Name] from Data Centre 254. Am I speaking with [Contact Name]?"

"Yes? Great. I'll be brief—we're building East Africa's premier data centre intelligence platform, and we'd like to feature [Facility Name] in our verified directory."

### Value Proposition (60 seconds)
"Think of us as the 'go-to resource' for anyone evaluating data centres in the region—CIOs, IT consultants, investors, and policymakers. We're profiling 50+ facilities across five countries, with a focus on accuracy and transparency."

"[Facility Name] is clearly a key player in [market segment], and having accurate information about your facility will help qualified buyers find you."

### Ask (30 seconds)
"We need about 15 minutes of your time to confirm technical specs, or if you prefer, you can simply share your latest brochure and we'll draft everything for your review."

"Would you be open to a brief call later this week, or is there someone else on your team who handles this type of partnership?"

### Objection Handling

**"We're too busy right now."**
"I completely understand. How about I send over a quick form you can complete at your convenience? It takes about 10 minutes, and we'll handle all the drafting."

**"Is this paid?"**
"The basic listing is completely free. We do offer premium profiles with enhanced features, but there's no obligation. Our goal is simply to have accurate data in the directory."

**"Send me information first."**
"Absolutely—I'll email you our one-pager and a link to our draft profile. Can I follow up with you on [specific day]?"

**"We don't share pricing publicly."**
"No problem at all. We can mark pricing as 'Available on Request.' We just want to ensure prospective customers can find you and understand your technical capabilities."

### Closing
"Excellent. I'll send over the email today, and I'll plan to follow up on [day]. Does [email address] work best for you?"

"Thank you so much for your time. Looking forward to working together!"

## Tips for Success

1. **Personalization Matters** – Reference specific achievements, recent expansions, or news about the facility
2. **Lead with Value** – Emphasize what they get (visibility, qualified leads) not what you need (data)
3. **Make It Easy** – Offer multiple participation options (call, form, email, existing materials)
4. **Respect Time** – Keep initial outreach under 200 words; offer 15-minute calls
5. **Follow Up Persistently** – Most responses come after the 2nd or 3rd touchpoint
6. **Leverage FOMO** – Mention competitors already participating (once you have them)
7. **Be Transparent** – Clearly state what's free vs. paid; no hidden agendas
