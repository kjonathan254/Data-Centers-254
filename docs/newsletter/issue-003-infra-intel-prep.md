## The item (draft copy, verify-at-publish)

**03 · Infrastructure Intelligence candidate:**

> The UPS is getting an AI-era rethink. Uptime Institute's Annual Outage
> Analysis 2026 (as reported by Data Center Knowledge) puts power issues behind
> more than one in five IT service outages over the past three years, with UPS
> failures the leading cause within power incidents at 39%, ahead of transfer
> switches (34%) and generators (28%). Hyperscalers are answering with
> rack-level battery backup, 800 V DC distribution and campus-scale battery
> storage configured as a UPS alternative, and Dell'Oro projects solid-state
> transformers will start weighing on UPS demand from 2029. The frame that
> matters for Kenya: with a grid already over 90% renewable by generation mix,
> the job is flipping from protecting the IT load from the grid to protecting
> the grid from the IT load. Kenyan facilities continue to run conventional
> centralized UPS and diesel designs, which remains the right baseline for
> today's loads. Analysis: /articles/ups-backup-power-kenyan-data-centres
