# DC254 Outreach Kit (Task 147)

Companion to `first-dollar-plan.md`. Everything the editor needs to run the
7-day sequence: positioning, channel order, templates, cadence, objection
handling, logging rules. House tone: precise, evidence-first, no hype, no em
dashes.

---

## 1. Positioning (memorise this)

"DC254 tracks every data centre in Kenya with evidence standards. We turn that
into weekly deal intelligence for the companies that build and supply them."

One-line proof: the live directory (data-centers-254.vercel.app) and the
morning brief with 15+ numbered sources. Never send a message without one
concrete, dated fact from it. The fact IS the hook.

## 2. Channel order (Kenya B2B reality)

1. WhatsApp (best reply rates in Nairobi business, keep it short, no attachments
   on first contact, one link max)
2. LinkedIn DM to a role, not a person-spam: target "Managing Director",
   "Business Development", "Sales Director", "Marketing Manager" at ICP-1
3. Email for OEM East Africa offices and international recruiters
4. Phone only after a reply (numbers from public contact pages only)

Send window: Tuesday to Thursday, 09:00-11:00 EAT. 20 sends per day maximum.
Log every send in `intelligence/prospects/2026-10-14-launch-list.csv` (status
column: SENT / REPLIED / CALL / SALE / NO).

## 3. Templates

### T1. WhatsApp opener (ICP-1 vendor, priority A)

> Hello, quick one. I run DC254, we track every data centre in Kenya
> (27 facilities, all evidence-checked). Example of what we saw first: iColo's
> NBO2 launch at 6.4 MW this September and iXAfrica's 18 MW NBOX1.2 under
> construction. I put out a weekly signal sheet (tenders, builds, expansions)
> for vendors like you. First issue attached as a free sample. If it is useful,
> the founding rate is KES 3,500/month for the first 10 companies. No reply
> needed if not relevant, I will not follow up twice.

Attach: `radar-issue-000-sample.md` exported as PDF (one page).

### T2. LinkedIn DM (ICP-1 or ICP-4)

> Hi [first name], I publish DC254, Kenya's data centre tracker (evidence-tiered
> directory + daily verified brief). Two things I thought you'd care about this
> week: KDEAP procurement closes 29 Oct, and the reported Microsoft ADC
> wind-down is reshaping DC talent availability. I write a weekly Radar for
> suppliers and recruiters working this market. Want the free sample issue?
> If it earns its keep, founding rate is KES 3,500/mo (10 slots).

### T3. Cold email (ICP-3 OEMs, ICP-4 international recruiters)

Subject: Kenya DC build signals (NBO2, NBOX1.2, KDEAP) - weekly verified sheet

Body: two short paragraphs. P1: who we are + the three why-now facts with
dates. P2: what the Radar contains, founding price, and the ask ("worth 15
minutes?"). Signature with site link. No images, no tracking pixels.

### T4. Operator soft-touch (ICP-2, the free ask)

> Hello [operator], your facility [name] is live on DC254's Kenya directory
> (link). We verify status and capacity evidence weekly. If anything is
> out of date or should link to your official page, tell me and I will fix it
> same day. No charge, we do this for all 14 operators on the platform.

(The upsell comes weeks later, after traffic proof: sponsored listing, Rack
Report sponsorship.)

### T5. The 3-day nudge (after any non-reply to T1/T2)

> Following up once with the sample issue attached, in case WhatsApp ate it.
> If it is not for you, a one-word "pass" works and I will close the file.

## 4. Objection handling (say the honest thing)

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "Send me info" | Send Radar Issue 000 PDF + one question: "which of these signals would have changed your week?" |
| "I already know the market" | "Nobody knew NBO2's 6.4 MW was live until we verified it. Speed is the product." |
| "Too expensive" | "One KDEAP tender missed costs more than a year of the Radar. Founding rate stays until 10 seats go." |
| "Who are you?" | "A verified tracker with 27 Kenyan facilities and a daily sourced brief. Here is the link, judge the work." |
| "We buy from DCD/Cloudscene" | "Those are global and lagging. We are Kenya-only and same-week, with announced-vs-operational discipline. Both can be true." |
| "Call me" | Call within the hour. Have Issue 000 open. Ask two questions, listen for 80 percent of the call. |

## 5. Daily logging rules

1. One row per prospect, one status at a time, dated notes ("2026-10-16 SENT
   T1", "2026-10-18 REPLIED wants invoice").
2. A sale is a SALE only when money moves. Intent is CALL.
3. Never scrape or buy personal contacts. Company channels and role targets
   only (house rule, also good law: Kenya DPA 2019).
4. Every fact stated in any message must exist in the evidence register or the
   directory with a source. If it is REPORTED, say "reported".

## 6. Weekly review (Day 7 and every Monday)

Count: sends, replies, calls, sales. Compute reply rate. If below 8 percent
after 60 sends: rewrite the opener using the best-performing fact, switch
priority order (vendors to recruiters), or narrow to 10 best-fit prospects and
go deeper. Kill rules prevent zombie outreach.
