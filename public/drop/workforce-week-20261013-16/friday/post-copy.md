## LinkedIn caption (attach partnership graphic)

Kenya's data centres will be run by people who have not all been trained yet.

This week DC254 introduced the roles, examined the skills question and mapped six
career pathways. One conclusion kept coming back: the talent pipeline is a partnership
project.

Industry knows which skills the floor requires. Universities, TVETs and technical
academies know how to build curricula. Students bring the ambition. The connection
between the three is where Kenya's workforce advantage will be built.

Partnerships between operators and training providers have already begun to appear.
The operators who invest in local talent are building something competitors cannot
import: a workforce that stays.

DC254 is opening its workforce research for partners. If you are an operator,
university, TVET or training academy, we want to hear from you about:

- Workforce research and evidence collection
- Mentorship and practical exposure
- Training partnerships and curriculum input

Contact: https://data-centers-254.vercel.app/contact
