## LinkedIn caption (attach carousel as document)

Kenya is building data centres faster than it is counting the people who will run them.

The buildings are tracked. The workforce is not.

DC254's State of Kenyan Data Centres 2026 records 27 tracked facilities, 20 operational,
and a 230 MW announced pipeline: 77 MW under construction, 53 MW committed, 100 MW early
stage.

Every one of those buildings will need power technicians, cooling engineers, network
specialists, security officers, cloud engineers and operations staff on site.

Here is what nobody has measured in Kenya: how many people that takes, which skills are
hardest to hire, and how fast local training can supply them.

We want to change that, with evidence.

If you work in the industry or teach it: which skills are hardest to develop or recruit
for? Your answers will shape a DC254 workforce briefing later this year.

Careers coverage: https://data-centers-254.vercel.app/careers
