## LinkedIn caption (attach carousel as document)

There is no single data-centre career.

Monday we introduced the people behind Kenya's data centres. Today: the six pathways
into the industry.

1 Power - UPS, generators, switchgear
2 Cooling - chillers, CRAC units, airflow
3 Networks - patch panels, fibre splicing, exchanges
4 Cloud and compute - servers, GPUs, platforms
5 Security - access control, CCTV, response
6 Operations - DCIM, monitoring, tickets

Each pathway starts from a different base: electrical, mechanical, telecom, software,
security or process discipline. TVET diplomas, degrees, apprenticeships and
certifications all lead in.

Which pathway should DC254 explain next? Tell us and we will build the explainer.
