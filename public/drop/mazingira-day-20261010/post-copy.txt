DC254 MAZINGIRA DAY — POST COPY (10 October 2026)
==================================================

A greener digital future is built one decision at a time.

Data centres power our digital lives, but their growth depends on real-world
resources: power, water, land, cooling systems and communities.

Kenya has a strong foundation. More than 90% of electricity generation comes
from renewable sources, with geothermal providing reliable baseload power.

But a sustainable digital economy requires more than clean-energy headlines.
It requires:

- Efficient cooling
- Responsible site selection
- Environmental assessment
- Reliable power delivery
- Water-conscious design
- Local skills, jobs and procurement
- Infrastructure that creates shared value

On Mazingira Day, the question is not only how fast digital infrastructure is
growing.

It is: How responsibly are we building it?
