## LinkedIn post copy

Africa has 3% of EMEA's data-centre capacity.

Where does Kenya sit?

DC Byte's latest EMEA analysis, reported by ITWeb, puts the region at 93 GW
once live facilities, construction, committed projects and early-stage
developments are counted together. Europe holds 88 percent. The Middle East
9 percent. Africa 3 percent.

The precision matters: 3 percent is Africa's share of the regional total,
not a share of global capacity, and not a forecast.

Kenya's numbers, staged the honest way, from the DC254 verified dataset:

- 10.5 MW published live IT load
- 42.9 MW built capacity at operational sites
- 230 MW tracked pipeline: 77 MW under construction, 53 MW committed,
  100 MW early stage
- About 93 percent renewable grid generation, geothermal baseload near
  45 percent
- Seven live subsea cable systems at the coast

Potential is not the same as operational capacity.

The 2027 test: how much of the 230 MW becomes operational.
