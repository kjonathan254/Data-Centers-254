## LinkedIn caption (primary post, attach carousel as document)

Kenya's data-centre industry needs more than buildings and servers. It needs people.

Behind every reliable data centre is a multidisciplinary team managing:

- Power and cooling
- UPS systems and generators
- Fibre and network connections
- Monitoring platforms
- Physical security
- Compliance
- Customer operations
- Construction and commissioning

The industry's future workforce will need more than one technical skill.

It will require people who understand how electrical systems, networks, cloud
infrastructure, cybersecurity and operations work together.

As Kenya expands its digital infrastructure, the opportunity is not only to build
more facilities.

It is to build a stronger local talent pipeline around them.

Which area would you choose?

Power. Cooling. Networks. Security. Cloud. Operations.
