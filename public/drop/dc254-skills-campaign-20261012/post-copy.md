## LinkedIn
Think data centres are only for software engineers?

There is no single data-centre career. Kenya's facilities — and the ones under
construction — run on six different discipline families:

1. Power and electrical systems
2. Cooling and mechanical systems
3. Networks and connectivity
4. IT and cloud operations
5. Security, safety and compliance (physical and cyber are different skills)
6. Projects, testing and commissioning

Role titles vary by employer — but the disciplines are the industry's backbone.

Which pathway should DC254 explain in more detail? Vote in the poll below, and
explore our careers coverage: https://data-centers-254.vercel.app/careers

## LinkedIn
What should students experience before entering a data centre?

Here is a proposed pathway — not an established national programme:

Classroom foundations → Practical exercises → Supervised exposure → Skills
assessment → Career preparation

Between each arrow sits something real: electrical and mechanical lab
exercises, structured-cabling practice, monitoring and troubleshooting
simulations, safety procedures and documentation, equipment demonstrations,
mentorship and supervised site visits, commissioning case studies.

Real facilities come with real constraints: supervision, safety controls,
confidentiality and operator permission come first.

Training institutions and employers: what practical learning opportunities are
available today — and where could industry collaboration help? Your examples
will inform DC254's workforce research.
