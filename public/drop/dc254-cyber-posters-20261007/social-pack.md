## LinkedIn post copy

Kenya recorded 11.1 billion detected cyber-threat events in FY 2025/26.

Not 11.1 billion breaches. Detected events: attempts, probes and malicious
sightings registered by the National KE-CIRT/CC, up 29.0% on the year before.

The useful question is not the number. It is what a country must build,
power, connect and staff to see that number at all.

The FY 2025/26 ladder from the regulator's own quarterly bulletins:
842M, then 4.56B (+441.27% spike quarter), then 3.37B, then 2.36B.
Total: 11,124,602,684.

83.1 million advisories issued (+60.8%). 144 member networks at KIXP with
2,985 Gbps of connected port capacity. 20 of 27 tracked facilities
operational, with a 230 MW announced pipeline.

Detection is a workload. It runs on racks, power, cooling and staffed
shifts, in buildings like the one in the poster.

The threat data and the construction data are the same story told twice.
