DC254 · Infrastructure Brief

Kenya recorded 11.1 billion detected cyber-threat events in FY 2025/26.

Not 11.1 billion breaches. Detected events, counted by national monitoring that exists, is staffed and publishes its methods. The infrastructure behind the count is the story: 20 operational facilities, 144 networks at KIXP, a 230 MW pipeline that absorbs both the threat curve and the load curve.
