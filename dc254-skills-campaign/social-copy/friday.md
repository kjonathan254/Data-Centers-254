Kenya's data-centre talent pipeline needs collaboration. Not one hero
institution — a pipeline.

Education builds technical foundations. Industry contributes practical
requirements and experience. Between them sit five places where collaboration
could happen:

- Curriculum alignment
- Practical exposure
- Mentorship
- Certification guidance
- Internships and entry-level pathways

with making workforce needs visible — with evidence.

opened an investigation into Kenya's data-centre workforce, and the responses
