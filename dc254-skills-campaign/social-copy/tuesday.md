## LinkedIn (hook inside the first ~2 lines)
Kenya is building data centres. Who will run them?

DC254's tracker lists 27 facilities in Kenya — 20 operational, 7 in
development. Every one of them depends on more than servers: power systems,
cooling, networks, and the people who operate them safely.

So here is the question this series is investigating: do Kenya's training
pathways match real operating requirements? We are not assuming an answer. We
are asking the people who know.

Operators and educators: what practical skills are hardest to develop or
recruit for in Kenya's data-centre industry? Specific examples welcome — they
will inform DC254's workforce research.
