The Terms, Decoded · Two Ways To Get Steam

Olkaria's steam is given. Fervo's is engineered.

Conventional hydrothermal · what Olkaria uses
01
A natural steam field. Magma heats groundwater trapped under cap rock; the resource made itself over geological time.
02
Wells tap the reservoir. Steam arrives under its own pressure and spins turbines; Kenya has run this way for four decades.
03
Proven at scale. 943.7 MW installed nationally, about 40 percent of the country's electricity.
Enhanced geothermal (EGS) · what Fervo builds at Cape Station
01
No natural steam field. Water is pumped into hot dry rock; the driller manufactures the reservoir the geology did not provide.
02
Horizontal wells and engineered fractures. Injection and production wells connect sideways through the heated rock.
03
Built like a factory. Standard 33 MW blocks, financed one PPA at a time; the first block delivered in September 2026.
